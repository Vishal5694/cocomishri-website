Chapter 1

It All Began With Her

Every brand has a beginning. Ours began with a tiny heartbeat and a whole new world.

When our daughter Adira came into our lives, everything changed. Our days got busier, our nights got shorter, and our hearts got fuller than we thought possible.

As her naming ceremony came closer, we wanted one simple thing for her: an outfit as soft and pure as she was. It had to be something she could wear comfortably in our arms, through all the blessings, hugs and photographs.

So we started searching. What we found surprised us. Most of it was shimmery, stiff and scratchy, made to look good in pictures but not to feel good on a baby's delicate skin. The few outfits that were truly soft and well made cost far more than most families could reasonably pay.

We kept asking each other: “Why should parents have to choose between comfort, quality and a fair price?”

That question stayed with us long after the ceremony was over. Slowly, it turned into a dream.

Chapter 2

Our Little Mishri

At home, Adira has never been just Adira. She is our Mishri.

Mishri is the little crystal of sugar that Indian homes have always kept close. It's offered in blessings, shared in celebrations and given to children with love. It's simple, pure and sweet, and it makes every moment a little better. That's exactly what she is to us.

So when our dream became a brand, the name had to carry her. Cocomishri was born. It's our way of keeping the sweetness she brought into our lives at the heart of everything we do.

Chapter 3

Made Before Our Own Eyes

Because Cocomishri carries our daughter's name, we knew we could never treat it like just another business.

Many manufacturers show beautiful samples, and then quality quietly slips once bulk production begins. We couldn't let that happen to something named after our child.

So we chose the harder, slower path. Every Cocomishri outfit is made under our own watch. We check the fabric, the stitching and the finish, and every piece gets the same love and attention as the very first one.

We ask this one question each time. If the answer isn't a happy yes, it doesn't reach you.

Chapter 4

Softness You Can Feel

A baby can't tell you when something itches. As parents, we've learned to read those little signs, and how much comfort really matters.

That's why fabric is where every Cocomishri piece begins: smooth, breathable and gentle on delicate skin, so little ones can crawl, play, nap and explore freely.

A comfortable child is free to simply be a child.

Chapter 5

  • Prices that are honest and fair for families
  • In every stitch, the same love we have for our own little one
